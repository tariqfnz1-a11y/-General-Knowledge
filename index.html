🌊 Open Sea & Mare Liberum

Open Sea: Beyond 200 nautical miles (≈370 km) from a coast, the sea is shared by all humanity. About 43% of Earth’s surface is international waters.

Mare Liberum: Latin for "Free sea". In the 17th century, Hugo Grotius argued the sea is too vast to be owned by one country.

🏜️ Terra Nullius

Latin for "land of no one". Today, it means land no recognised state owns. Historically, colonial powers misused this idea, calling inhabited lands "empty" to take them over unfairly.

🐧 Marie Byrd Land, Antarctica

Over 1.6 million km², the largest unclaimed area on Earth. The Antarctic Treaty (1959) set the continent aside for peace and science. It's so remote nobody has bothered to claim it!

🇪🇬 Bir Tawil (Egypt-Sudan Border)

Britain drew two different borders (1899 and 1902). Both Egypt and Sudan want the valuable Halaib Triangle, so they each pick the border line that gives it to them. As a result, neither claims the useless Bir Tawil desert!

🚩 Liberland & Gornja Siga

Gornja Siga: A small patch between Croatia and Serbia, left over after the Danube river was straightened.

Liberland: A "micronation" founded in 2015 by Vít Jedlička. No official country recognises it.
